Private local AI that gets better the more people use it.

Tessera Studio runs Gemma 4 12B on your Mac. Text, vision, audio, and speech. Your data never leaves the device. As more people use Tessera, the model reasons better on harder problems, the drafters get more accurate, the engine gets faster, and next-token prediction sharpens. The recipe and the signed receipt are public; the data method is private. The improved model ships to every Tessera user, not just whoever paid for it.

What you get

Four things, in plain language.

Local, private, on your Mac.

Text, vision, audio, and speech. Your data stays on your device. No cloud, no telemetry that can identify you, no model that phones home.

It gets better the more people use it.

Better reasoning on harder problems, sharper hybrid drafters, faster engine, SOTA-class next-token prediction. The flywheel runs on a sanitized baseline during idle time.

A model you can trust is improving.

The recipe is public. The signed receipt is on every release. The sanitization method is private; the audit trail is not.

Free, with a clear path for commercial use.

Source-available engine. CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 trained models. Commercial licensing is a conversation, not a paywall. julian@tribunus.dev

Who this is for

One primary audience. Two adjacent. One peripheral.

Primary

Privacy-conscious individuals

You don't want cloud AI. You want local, on your machine, on your data. Tessera Studio is the on-ramp.

Adjacent

Apple Silicon power users

You want the ANE prefill, the IOSurface hand-off, the on-device throughput. Tessera dispatches across CPU, GPU, and ANE dynamically.

Adjacent

Inference engineers

You run llama.cpp and want calibrated quantization with receipts. Tessera is a fork of llama.cpp that adds the kernel-fidelity loop.

Peripheral

Quant researchers

You want the evolutionary AWQ, the per-tensor GA, the regime-routed archive. That's on the architecture page.

If you don't care about local inference, this isn't for you. If you need bit-for-bit drop-in compatibility with stock llama.cpp for Tessera-quantized artifacts, this isn't for you (vanilla quants work anywhere; Tessera-locked artifacts need the Tessera runtime).

Runnability

A note on which artifacts run where. GGUF models quantized by Tessera use Tessera-specific types that only the Tessera dequant kernel can read. Vanilla GGUFs (standard quantizations of upstream models) work on both stock llama.cpp and Tessera.

ArtifactStock llama.cppTesseraWhy
Vanilla GGUF (upstream quant) Yes Yes Standard GGUF types, no Tessera-specific tensors
Tessera-quantized model (T640 / T640_3D) No Yes Tessera-specific GGUF types require the Tessera dequant kernel
Tessera-trained model No Yes Trained and quantized via Tessera, so Tessera-locked at the dequant layer

Tessera-locked isn't a liability — it's the receipt. The artifact is shaped to the kernel that graded it; the kernel ships in the fork; the fork is source-available.
