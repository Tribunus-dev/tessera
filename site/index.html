Guided tour · Stop 01 of 04 · Open the app

Private local AI that gets better the more people use it.

This is Tessera Studio, the app that runs Tessera on your Mac. Two models on the shelf: one for text, vision, and audio, one for speech. Your data never leaves the device. Scroll, and the app does the talking.

02
Guided tour · Stop 02 of 04 · Ask it anything

One conversation, three modalities. Privately.

A photo, your voice, plain text - the unified model takes all three in one conversation and answers on the spot. No account, no cloud, no telemetry that can identify you. The prompt and the answer stay on your machine.

Inside the Playground →
03
Guided tour · Stop 03 of 04 · Leave it running

It improves while you sleep.

Opt in once. While your machine idles - plugged in, on Wi-Fi, screen off - Tessera trains on a sanitized baseline of your usage. The raw inputs never leave. Your delta ships with your consent, and the improved model ships to every Tessera user.

The flywheel, in detail →
04
Guided tour · Stop 04 of 04 · Audit everything

Nothing ships without receipts.

Every Tessera model is calibrated and audited quantization: the recipe and the eval results are public on every release, signed into the artifact itself. The sanitization method stays private. The contract is public; the method is not.
